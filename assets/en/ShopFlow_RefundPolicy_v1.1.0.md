# ShopFlow Refund Policy (v1.1.0)

> A fictional document for demonstration purposes. ShopFlow is a fictional online store.

## 1. Request window
- Standard members: a refund can be requested within 30 days of the purchase date.
- VIP members: a refund can be requested within 60 days of the purchase date.
- Orders past the window cannot be requested.

## 2. Refund reason
- A reason is required when a refund is requested (1-200 characters).
- Legacy mobile apps (3.1-3.3) can still submit without a reason until 2026-12-01.
- From 2026-12-02, legacy apps can no longer submit refund requests; please use the web.

## 3. High-value refund approval
- A refund above KRW 1,000,000 is processed only after admin approval.
- Until it is approved, the refund is not processed and no approval e-mail is sent.
- A refund of exactly KRW 1,000,000 is approved automatically unless the order is in transit.
- An order that is in transit always needs admin approval, whatever the amount.

## 4. Requests that cannot be submitted
- Cancelled orders
- Orders past the request window
- Requests above the remaining refundable amount (a partial refund is possible within the remaining amount)
